# 医学讨论段落自然化示例

## 原文

> These results demonstrate that the rehabilitation program may potentially improve upper limb
> function in stroke patients. Furthermore, the intervention group showed better outcomes than
> the control group. Moreover, these findings indicate that early intervention might possibly be
> beneficial. Nevertheless, further studies are needed.

## 可观察的问题

- “demonstrate”“may potentially”在同一句中造成证据强度冲突；
- furthermore、moreover、nevertheless 密集但没有增加逻辑；
- “better outcomes”“beneficial”过于空泛；
- 原文没有效应量、时间窗或具体量表，不能在编辑时补入。

## 修订稿（仅语言编辑，科学强度问题待作者确认）

> These results demonstrate that the rehabilitation program may improve upper limb
> function in stroke patients. The intervention group showed better outcomes than
> the control group. These findings indicate that early intervention might be
> beneficial. Further studies are needed.
