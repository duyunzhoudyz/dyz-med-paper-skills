Across all 13 trials, DerSimonian-Laird random-effects pooling yielded a risk ratio of 0.49 (95% CI 0.34–0.69; 95% prediction interval 0.14–1.77), with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 92.1%, Q = 152.23, tau2 = 0.3088). [claim:C001] [evidence:E001]

## Heterogeneity Exploration and Leave-One-Out Sensitivity

Stratification by latitude explained a substantial share of heterogeneity: high-latitude trials (>=35 deg, k=6) had pooled RR 0.30 (95% CI 0.20–0.44, I2=75.4%), whereas low/mid-latitude trials (<35 deg, k=7) had pooled RR 0.76 (95% CI 0.58–1.00, I2=72.0%). In leave-one-out sensitivity analyses across all 13 trials, the pooled RR ranged from 0.45 to 0.54. [claim:C002] [evidence:E001]
