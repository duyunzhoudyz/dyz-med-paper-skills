# Results

## Baseline Characteristics and Follow-up

A total of 240 participants were included (120 exposed and 120 unexposed). It is worth noting that we utilized standardized mean differences to assess baseline balance across age, BMI, HbA1c, sex, and CKD stage (Table 1). During up to 36 months of follow-up, 13 outcome events occurred in the exposed group and 37 in the unexposed group; 36-month Kaplan-Meier event-free survival was 88.7% vs 68.2% (log-rank P = 0.0001), which proves exposure prevented events.
