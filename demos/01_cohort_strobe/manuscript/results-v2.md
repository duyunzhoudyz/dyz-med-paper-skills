# Results

## Baseline Characteristics and Follow-up

A total of 240 participants were included (120 exposed and 120 unexposed). Baseline balance across age, BMI, HbA1c, sex, and CKD stage was summarized using absolute standardized mean differences without baseline hypothesis testing (Table 1). During up to 36 months of follow-up, 13 outcome events occurred in the exposed group and 37 in the unexposed group; 36-month Kaplan-Meier event-free survival was 88.7% vs 68.2% (log-rank P = 0.0001), indicating an unadjusted association that cannot establish causality alone in an observational cohort.
