# Diagnostic Accuracy Results (STARD-2015 Demo)

## Cross-Tabulation and Accuracy Estimates

Full 2x2 cross-tabulation (TP=94, FP=47, FN=16, TN=143) and metric intervals are reported in Table 1 (`results/confusion-and-metrics.md`) and Figure 1 (`figures/figure1-roc.svg`). Among 300 evaluated cases (prevalence 36.7%; 110 reference-positive, 190 reference-negative), the index test at the prespecified 0.50 cut-off achieved sensitivity 85.5% (95% CI 77.7%–90.8%), specificity 75.3% (95% CI 68.7%–80.9%), PPV 66.7%, NPV 89.9%, LR+ 3.455, LR- 0.193, and DeLong ROC-AUC 0.873 (95% CI 0.833–0.912).
