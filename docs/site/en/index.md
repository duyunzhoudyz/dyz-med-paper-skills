## Built around clinical research in China

Discuss questions and revisions in Chinese before preparing Chinese or English prose. The workflow can account for departmental case data, follow-up constraints, supervisor feedback, graduation requirements, journal scope and publication budgets. Most skill instructions and supporting materials are in Chinese; this site provides an English entry point.

You do not need to memorize skill names. A useful first request is:

> Here are my results table, verified references and discussion draft. Map the main findings, then revise the discussion. Separate evidence from interpretation, list unsupported claims and explain the changes in Chinese.

An existing draft can go straight to diagnosis and revision. Reviewer feedback can go straight to a response plan. One invocation does not automatically complete a paper.

## Keep the files and the reasoning

Each skill bundles a `SKILL.md` file with relevant templates, references and local scripts. Store research inputs and outputs in a separate paper workspace. Outlines, drafts, literature records and revision logs remain available for supervisors and collaborators. The built-in exporter produces basic Word review drafts.

## Current scope

This is a public beta. Database searches require access, and the built-in online search script covers PubMed only. Statistical execution needs an appropriate environment and approval of the specific plan. Real-host behavior, sustained writing and independent medical quality have not all been validated. Teaching examples are synthetic, not publication outcomes or user testimonials.
